# ScreenPulse brand

The identity is drawn from the hardware the product talks through: aluminium, black
keycaps, the white backlight and one small green LED.

- **Name**: ScreenPulse (one word, capital S and P).
- **Mark**: three rows of dots – 1, 2, 3 – the blink code for A, B and C. The first dot is the lit LED. Sources: `website/src/components/Logo.astro`, `website/public/favicon.svg`, `app/Tools/make-icon.swift`, `app/Sources/ScreenPulse/BrandIcon.swift`.
- **Colour**: aluminium `#e3e5e8`, plate `#f4f5f6`, keycap `#1a1b1e`, ink `#131417`, muted `#555961`, LED green `#2fd671`. The green is used at LED size only (dots, indicators), never as a fill or headline colour. For green text on light backgrounds use `#0d7a3a`.
- **Type**: Schibsted Grotesk (variable), weights 400/500/600. Tight tracking on headlines.
- **Shapes**: buttons are keys (9 px radius, a hard bottom edge). Sequences are shown as rows of dots rather than numerals.
- **Motion**: light turning on and off. One demonstration plays when the page loads; everything else responds to the visitor.
- **Voice**: plain and exact. Say what works on which Mac. Never promise hardware support that has not been tested, and never present the product as a tool for exams.
