Get it

Get your Gemini key

ScreenPulse needs a key from Google to answer your questions. Getting one takes about five minutes, and you only do it once.

What is a key?

A key is a long secret password. It tells Google that the questions come from you. ScreenPulse keeps your key safe on your Mac.

Before you start

  • You need a Google account. If you have Gmail, you have one.
  • You need about five minutes.
  • Step 6 asks for a payment card. If the card is not yours, ask a grown-up first.
  1. 1

    Open Google AI Studio

    Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.

    You should see: a page called API Keys.

  2. 2

    Click “Create API key”

    Look for the button that says Create API key at the top right, and click it.

  3. 3

    Name the key and create it

    Give the key a name you will recognise, for example ScreenPulse. Google also asks for a project. A project is just a folder where your key lives: keep the one Google suggests (often Default Gemini Project). Then click Create key.

  4. 4

    Copy your key

    Your key is one long line of letters and numbers. Click Copy key.

    Keep it secret. Do not send it to anyone, post it online or save it in a shared document. If it ever leaks, delete it on the same page and make a new one.

  5. 5

    Paste it into ScreenPulse

    1. Click the ScreenPulse icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen, and choose Settings…
    2. Find the box called API key under Gemini, and paste your key (press ⌘ and V).
    3. Click Save, then Check key.

    You should see: a green message that says the key works.

  6. 6

    Recommended: turn on billing and set a limit

    A key works straight away without paying. Free keys have small limits, though, and Google may use what you send on a free key to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. Keys with billing turned on are treated as private, and have much more room. Check Google’s current terms to be sure.

    Paid does not mean expensive. A question costs a small fraction of a cent up to a bit under one cent. You can also tell Google the most you are willing to pay each month, so there are no surprises.

    1. In AI Studio, click Billing in the menu on the left (or Set up billing next to your key) and follow the steps to add a payment card.
    2. Click Spend in the menu on the left, then Edit spend cap, and set a monthly limit that you are comfortable with. A small amount is plenty to start.
    3. Come back to ScreenPulse and press Check key again.

    Button names change from time to time. Look for the words “billing” and “spend cap”.

  7. 7

    Ask your first question

    Point your mouse at something on the screen and press ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ Space together. That is the Control, Option, Command and Space keys.

    The first time, your Mac asks if ScreenPulse may record the screen. Click Allow, then quit ScreenPulse from its menu bar icon and open it again.

If something goes wrong

“The key was not accepted”
Copy the key again. Make sure you copied the whole line and no spaces before or after it.
“Too many requests”
Wait a minute and try again. Free keys have small limits. Turning on billing (step 6) gives you more room.
“Your credits are used up”
Add funds or raise your limit in Google’s billing page.
ScreenPulse cannot see the screen
Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Screen & System Audio Recording. Switch ScreenPulse on. Quit ScreenPulse and open it again.
Nothing happens when I press the keys
Check that the ScreenPulse icon is in the menu bar. Press all four keys together: Control, Option, Command and Space.

Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub. What you write there is public, so never paste your key.
